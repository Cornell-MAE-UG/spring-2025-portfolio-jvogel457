## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/headshot.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is {{ site.name }}, and I am a Mechanical Engineering MEng student at Cornell University, concentrating in biomechanics. After completing my undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering at Cornell, I continued into the MEng program to explore the intersection of mechanical engineering and human health.

Much of my undergraduate experience was shaped by Cornell Electric Vehicles (CEV), where I served as the mechanical lead for the team’s 2026 Urban Concept Car. Through CEV, I gained extensive hands-on experience designing, manufacturing, and testing mechanical systems, and learned how to take an idea from an initial concept through a functioning product.

I am now building on that mechanical engineering foundation through my work in the Hasani Lab, where I am helping develop a device that conditions immune cells through mechanical stimulation. This experience has allowed me to apply my mechanical design skills to a biomedical problem while learning more about the interaction between engineered systems and biological processes.

My project-team experience has also been complemented by internships at Lutron Electronics and General Motors, where I gained experience working within real engineering organizations and learned how products are developed and problems are solved in industry. At both companies, I was able to take ownership of projects and contribute beyond the classroom.

I am particularly interested in full-time opportunities in biomedical device engineering, but I am open to opportunities across industries. Ultimately, I want to build products that improve people's lives—whether that means developing biomedical technologies, improving transportation, or solving other practical problems through thoughtful engineering. I will graduate in May 2027 and am currently seeking full-time engineering opportunities.
